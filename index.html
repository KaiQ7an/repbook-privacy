Repbook Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Repbook is a strength-training log for iPhone, developed by Kaiqian Xue. This policy explains what information Repbook handles, where it goes, and how you can remove it. In short: your workouts stay on your iPhone, an account is optional, and nothing is used for advertising or tracking.

Information that stays on your iPhone

  • Training data — routines, exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, notes, goals, body measurements you enter, and app settings. These are stored only on your device. Signing in does not upload them, and Repbook has no server that receives them. Each account you sign in with has its own training data on the iPhone, kept apart from other accounts and from the signed-out profile.
  • Form Check — when you use Form Check, the camera image is analysed on your device to count repetitions and estimate squat depth. Video is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Training insights — on iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, progression suggestions are written by Apple's on-device language model; on other iPhones, by rules built into the app. Either way your training data is processed on the device and is not sent to Apple or anyone else.
  • Widgets, Live Activities and notifications — the rest timer, home-screen widget and reminders are produced on your device from your training data.
  • Diagnostics — if the app crashes, Apple's MetricKit may give Repbook a diagnostic report. It is kept on your device and is sent only if you choose to share it.

You can export your training data or delete it at any time in Settings. Deleting removes the routines, workouts, your own exercises, goals, body measurements and weigh-ins of the profile you are using. Deleting the app also deletes it.

Apple Health

If you turn on saving to Health, Repbook writes each finished session to Apple Health as a strength training workout, and reads only enough to check which of its own workouts it has already saved. Health data is never shared with anyone, never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and never stored in iCloud by Repbook. You can turn this off in Repbook's Settings or in the Health app.

Optional account

You can use Repbook without an account. If you create one, sign-in is provided by Google's Firebase Authentication, and, if you choose it, Sign in with Google. The account holds your email address, your name if you provide one or your sign-in provider shares it, and a user ID. It is used only to sign you in and verify your email. As part of providing sign-in, Google may process technical information such as your IP address; see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

You can change your name and email in the app, and you can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account. Deleting the account removes it from Firebase, and you choose whether that account's training data on the iPhone is deleted or kept in the signed-out profile.

If you sign in while the iPhone holds training data saved while signed out, Repbook asks once whether to move it into the account. Nothing moves without your answer.

Equipment Scan (optional)

Equipment Scan identifies gym equipment from a photo. Nothing is sent until you take or choose a photo and then confirm sending it. When you confirm, that one photo is resized and stripped of its metadata (such as location) and sent through Google's Firebase AI Logic to Google's Gemini model, which returns the type of equipment. Repbook does not store the photo or add it to your training history. Avoid photos that include people or personal information. Google processes the photo under its own terms; see the Gemini API terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

To protect this service from misuse, Firebase App Check confirms that requests come from a genuine copy of Repbook. You can always choose equipment manually instead, which sends nothing.

What Repbook does not do

  • No advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics service, and no selling or sharing of your information.
  • No use of your information for any purpose other than the feature you are using.

Your choices

  • Camera, Health and notification access can be turned off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Sign out, or delete your account, in Settings → Account.
  • Export or delete your training data in Settings.
